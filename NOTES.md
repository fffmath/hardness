# Reduction formalization

The LaTeX sources `paper/src/chapter/pre.tex` and
`paper/src/chapter/principal-ideal.tex` are the specification. Indices are
zero-based. Integer coefficient vectors carry the ordinary squared Euclidean
norm. Runtime complexity and NP membership are deliberately outside the scope.

## Files and theorem names

All names below are in namespace `Hardness` unless qualified otherwise.

- `Lattice.lean`: `Coeff`, `sqNorm`, `CVPYes`, and
  `MinimumSquaredDistance`. The last predicate asserts both an attained value
  and a universal lower bound, so it states a minimum without an infimum API.
  `sqNorm_pos` supplies the integral residual gap.
- `X3C.lean`: `X3CInstance` has an indexed collection of triples on `Fin (3*q)`.
  `X3CInstance.IsExactCover` counts exactly one selected incident index per
  universe element. `MaskedSystem.Feasible` includes both the Boolean mask
  condition and the incidence equations. `cover_iff_feasible`,
  `hasExactCover_iff_hasSolution`, and `exactCoverEquivFeasible` prove the X3C
  specialization and the bijection, including repeated triples as separate
  indices.
- `Sidon.lean`: `SidonPositions` is the interface used by the reduction.
  `SidonPositions.cubic` constructs positions with a proved strict order and
  injective nonzero ordered differences. `cubic_zero` and `cubic_size` give
  the origin and polynomial bound. This is a proved alternative to paper
  Lemma `lem:sidon`: alpha_j=(2n^2+1)j+j^2, with cubic size. The paper's
  quadratic Erdős–Turán formula is also defined as `erdosTuranAlpha`, with
  origin and strict-monotonicity proofs; its difference theorem is not
  claimed. No unproved Sidon assumption is used by `x3cToCVP`.
- `Coordinates.lean`: `SidonPositions.L`, `ρ`, `hExp`, `crossExp`, `N`.
  `hExp_injective`, `crossExp_injective`, `row_ne_cross`, and the `_lt_N`
  bounds are the support and no-wrap arguments in `lem:boolean-identity`
  and the subsequent unnumbered distinct-monomials lemma.
- `Cyclotomic.lean`: `Cyclotomic.R d` is `Fin d → ℤ`; `Cyclotomic.mul`
  is explicit negacyclic convolution. `principalIdealLattice g` is exactly
  the additive image `{g*q | q}`, not a subgroup generated by a larger
  family. `interleave`, `exists_interleave`, `interleave_injective`, and
  `sqNorm_interleave` give the unique orthogonal even/odd decomposition.
  `sqNorm_sparse` proves orthogonality for arbitrary injectively positioned
  sparse sums. No ring or domain instance is postulated; association with
  a polynomial quotient is not formalized.
- `Blocks.lean`: finite parity reindexing, `even_mul`, `mul_y` (in
  `Cyclotomic.lean`), and `shift_interleave` prove the parity algebra used
  in equation `(block-product)`.
- `Rounding.lean`: `rounding_gap` is the integer-scaled statement
  `4*Phi >= omega+4` for every noncanonical pair. Both coordinate rounding
  and the strict discrete gap are proved.
- `Norms.lean`: `sqNorm_monomial_mul` proves the signed-permutation
  isometry, `sparse_mul_bound` is squared Young's inequality, and
  `block_norm_lower_bound` is the reverse triangle estimate in Case 2 of
  `lem:admissible`. `realVec` is used for analysis only; the output instance
  remains integral.
- `Penalty.lean`: `penalty_separation` is the final scalar square-root
  estimate in Case 2 of `lem:admissible`.
- `Reduction.lean`: `ReductionData` contains the masked system, proved
  positions, and positive sizes. Its definitions `W`, `ν`, `h`, `t`, `a`,
  `u`, `s`, `P`, `g`, `T`, `Δ`, and `encode` implement the paper's formulas.
  `generator_mask_independent` proves the ideal is independent of the mask.
  `ceilSqrt` uses `Nat.sqrt`; `ceilSqrt_eq` identifies it with the real
  ceiling in the paper, so the output construction is executable.
  `h_support_card`, `ha_expansion`, and `boolean_identity` prove the
  distinct-monomials and Boolean norm identities.
  `generator_product` and `block_product` give the two-block multiplication
  formula. `mask_norm`, `decode_rounded`, `discrete_gap`,
  `admissible_allowed`, and `admissible_forbidden` implement both parts of
  Lemma `lem:admissible`. `g_ne_zero` uses its coefficient at y.
- `FullRank.lean`: `ReductionData.mul_injective` uses P>W and Young's
  inequality, rather than assuming a domain instance.
  `multiplicationEquiv` and `multiplicationBasis` exhibit the ideal as a
  free integer lattice of rank d with its multiplication basis.
- `Main.lean`: `ReductionData.compiler_correct`, `compiler_yes`,
  `compiler_no`, and `feasibleEquivClosest` are the mathematical
  correctness, minimum, gap, and correspondence assertions of
  `thm:compiler`. `x3cToCVP` outputs a `PrincipalCVPInstance`, carrying a
  proof that d is a power of two, g is nonzero, and Delta is nonnegative.
  `x3c_correct`, `x3c_yes_gap`, `x3c_no_gap`, and
  `exactCoverEquivClosest` are the X3C specialization of
  `thm:cyclotomic-cvp`. The bijection sends a cover to g*a_xi(y^2).
- `Dimensions.lean`: `SidonPositions.N_le`, `ReductionData.dimension_le`,
  and `x3c_dimension_le` bound the ambient dimension explicitly.

## Size and scope

The coordinate construction is the paper's construction relative to a Sidon
sequence. The canonical map uses the cubic construction, so its dimension is
O(M(n^3+1)), rather than the paper's sharper O(Mn^2). This change affects the
size bound only; h, t, the parity penalty, threshold, and squared-distance gap
use the paper's formulas. `W_le`, `weight_le`, `cross_card`, and `t_norm`
provide additional finite-size and norm calculations. `Reduction.lean` also
has a documented size/explicitness section. No complexity class, NP-completeness,
or computational running-time assertion is encoded as a theorem.

The representation is a concrete coefficient model with explicitly defined
negacyclic multiplication, not a transported quotient ring. General ring laws,
quotient equivalence, irreducibility, and canonical-embedding norm scaling are
not part of this development. Full rank for the constructed generator is
proved directly.

## Proof audit

- `lake build` succeeds with the specified toolchain and existing Mathlib.
- There are **no remaining `sorry` placeholders** and no new `axiom`
  declarations anywhere in the library.
- `#print axioms` for `boolean_identity`, `block_product`, both admissibility
  lemmas, `x3c_correct`, both X3C gap theorems, `exactCoverEquivClosest`, and
  `multiplicationBasis` reports only Lean's usual `propext`,
  `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. In particular, none uses `sorryAx`.
- An executable one-triple exact-cover example gives dimension 8, threshold
  2116, and squared distance 2116 for the encoded cover. This checks that
  the actual constructor and coefficient arithmetic evaluate, in addition
  to the general correctness proofs.
- The project configuration, manifest, and toolchain were not modified;
  neither dependency updates nor cache downloads were used.
