# Reduction formalization

The LaTeX sources `paper/src/chapter/pre.tex` and
`paper/src/chapter/principal-ideal.tex` are the specification. Indices are
zero-based. Integer coefficient vectors carry the ordinary squared Euclidean
norm. Runtime complexity and NP membership are deliberately outside the scope.

## Files and theorem names

All names below are in namespace `Hardness` unless qualified otherwise.

- `Lattice.lean`: `Coeff`, `sqNorm`, `CVPYes`, and
  `MinimumSquaredDistance`. The last predicate asserts both an attained value
  and a universal lower bound, so it states a minimum without an infimum API.
  `sqNorm_pos` supplies the integral residual gap.
- `X3C.lean`: `X3CInstance` has an indexed collection of triples on `Fin (3*q)`.
  `X3CInstance.IsExactCover` counts exactly one selected incident index per
  universe element. `MaskedSystem.Feasible` includes both the Boolean mask
  condition and the incidence equations. `cover_iff_feasible`,
  `hasExactCover_iff_hasSolution`, and `exactCoverEquivFeasible` prove the X3C
  specialization and the bijection, including repeated triples as separate
  indices.
- `Sidon.lean`: `SidonPositions` is the interface used by the reduction.
  `erdosTuranAlpha p j = 2*p*j + j^2 % p` is the exact zero-based formula
  of paper Lemma `lem:sidon`. `erdosTuran_differences` proves uniqueness of
  all nonzero ordered differences for prime p, 2<p, and n≤p. Its integer
  quotient argument recovers k-j, then cancellation in `ZMod p` recovers
  j and k. `exists_erdosTuranPrime`, `erdosTuranPrime` (a `Nat.find` search),
  and `erdosTuranPrime_interval` obtain an odd prime in [n,2n) for n≥2
  from Bertrand. Inputs n≤1 use p=3 and only the zero position.
  `SidonPositions.erdosTuran`, `erdosTuran_origin`, `erdosTuran_size`,
  and `erdosTuran_size_lower` give the complete construction, origin, and
  bounds 2n(n-1)≤c≤4n². The proved cubic alternative is retained.
- `Coordinates.lean`: `SidonPositions.L`, `ρ`, `hExp`, `crossExp`, `N`.
  `hExp_injective`, `crossExp_injective`, `row_ne_cross`, and the `_lt_N`
  bounds are the support and no-wrap arguments in `lem:boolean-identity`
  and the subsequent unnumbered distinct-monomials lemma.
- `Cyclotomic.lean`: `Cyclotomic.R d` is `Fin d → ℤ`; `Cyclotomic.mul`
  is explicit negacyclic convolution. `principalIdealLattice g` is exactly
  the additive image `{g*q | q}`, not a subgroup generated by a larger
  family. `interleave`, `exists_interleave`, `interleave_injective`, and
  `sqNorm_interleave` give the unique orthogonal even/odd decomposition.
  `sqNorm_sparse` proves orthogonality for arbitrary injectively positioned
  sparse sums. No ring or domain instance is postulated; association with
  a polynomial quotient is proved in `Quotient.lean`.
- `Blocks.lean`: finite parity reindexing, `even_mul`, `mul_y` (in
  `Cyclotomic.lean`), and `shift_interleave` prove the parity algebra used
  in equation `(block-product)`.
- `Rounding.lean`: `rounding_gap` is the integer-scaled statement
  `4*Phi >= omega+4` for every noncanonical pair. Both coordinate rounding
  and the strict discrete gap are proved.
- `Norms.lean`: `sqNorm_monomial_mul` proves the signed-permutation
  isometry, `sparse_mul_bound` is squared Young's inequality, and
  `block_norm_lower_bound` is the reverse triangle estimate in Case 2 of
  `lem:admissible`. `realVec` is used for analysis only; the output instance
  remains integral.
- `Penalty.lean`: `penalty_separation` is the final scalar square-root
  estimate in Case 2 of `lem:admissible`.
- `Reduction.lean`: `ReductionData` contains the masked system, proved
  positions, and positive sizes. Its definitions `W`, `ν`, `h`, `t`, `a`,
  `u`, `s`, `P`, `g`, `T`, `Δ`, and `encode` implement the paper's formulas.
  `generator_mask_independent` proves the ideal is independent of the mask.
  `ceilSqrt` uses `Nat.sqrt`; `ceilSqrt_eq` identifies it with the real
  ceiling in the paper, so the output construction is executable.
  `h_support_card`, `ha_expansion`, and `boolean_identity` prove the
  distinct-monomials and Boolean norm identities.
  `generator_product` and `block_product` give the two-block multiplication
  formula. `mask_norm`, `decode_rounded`, `discrete_gap`,
  `admissible_allowed`, and `admissible_forbidden` implement both parts of
  Lemma `lem:admissible`. `g_ne_zero` uses its coefficient at y.
- `FullRank.lean`: `ReductionData.mul_injective` uses P>W and Young's
  inequality, rather than assuming a domain instance.
  `multiplicationEquiv` and `multiplicationBasis` exhibit the ideal as a
  free integer lattice of rank d with its multiplication basis.
- `Main.lean`: `ReductionData.compiler_correct`, `compiler_yes`,
  `compiler_no`, and `feasibleEquivClosest` are the mathematical
  correctness, minimum, gap, and correspondence assertions of
  `thm:compiler`. `x3cToCVP` outputs a `PrincipalCVPInstance`, carrying a
  proof that d is a power of two, g is nonzero, and Delta is nonnegative.
  `x3c_correct`, `x3c_yes_gap`, `x3c_no_gap`, and
  `exactCoverEquivClosest` are the X3C specialization of
  `thm:cyclotomic-cvp`. The bijection sends a cover to g*a_xi(y^2).
- `Dimensions.lean`: `SidonPositions.N_le`, `ReductionData.dimension_le`,
  and `x3c_dimension_le` prove `(x3cToCVP I).d ≤ 4*(3*I.q)*(8*I.n²+1)`.
- `Quotient.lean`: `Cyclotomic.QuotRing d` is definitionally the genuine
  `Polynomial ℤ ⧸ Ideal.span {X^d+1}` (via Mathlib's `AdjoinRoot`).
  `toQuot_eq_mk` identifies `toQuot` with the class of the coefficient
  polynomial. `coefficientBasis`, `quotEquiv`, and `toQuot_bijective`
  give its ℤ-linear bijection for d>0 using monic degree-<d representatives.
  `toQuot_mul` proves that explicit negacyclic convolution is quotient
  multiplication. `mem_principalIdealLattice_iff` and
  `principalIdealLattice_eq_comap` identify the coefficient lattice with
  the preimage of `Ideal.span {toQuot g}`. `x3c_correct_quotient` restates
  `thm:cyclotomic-cvp` using that actual ideal and coefficient distances.

- `Cyclic.lean`: `Cyclic.S d` is the actual `Polynomial ℤ` quotient by
  `X^(2*d)-1`. `concat`, `toRing`, and `coeffEquiv` implement its two
  contiguous coefficient blocks and their unique ring representatives.
  `generator_image` and `lift_image` identify the coefficient formulas
  with the paper's G and T-hat. `anti_copy_mul` uses
  `Cyclotomic.polynomial_mul_difference_dvd` to transport negacyclic
  products into this ring. `product_image` and `product_distance` give
  equation `eq:principal-crt-distance`. `sqNorm_lift_sub`, `small_iff`,
  and `smallEquiv` prove the factor-eight norm identity and unique
  small-point correspondence of Lemma `lem:principal-crt`.
  `Cyclic.lattice` is the coefficient preimage of `Ideal.span {toRing G}`.
  `multiplicationBasis` retains its general injectivity-based interface.
  `principalMultiplicationBasis` and `principal_full_rank` remove that
  hypothesis for every nonzero power-of-two cyclotomic generator, using
  the integral plus/minus block argument and B≠0 (in particular H≥0,
  B²>4H). `X3CInstance.cyclicBasis` now uses the general domain theorem. `penalty` is
  exactly 2 ceil(sqrt(H)) + 1, with H=Delta+4 in `cyclicPenalty`.
  `x3cToCyclicCVP`, `x3c_cyclic_power_two`, `x3c_cyclic_correct`,
  `x3c_cyclic_yes_gap`, and `x3c_cyclic_no_gap` give the mathematical
  reduction in `thm:cyclic-cvp`, with dimension 2d and squared-distance
  gap 8 Delta versus 8 Delta+32.

- `GeneralFullRank.lean`: `Cyclotomic.pow_two_cyclotomic` identifies
  X^(2^k)+1 with Φ_(2·2^k) over every commutative ring, including k=0.
  `modulus_irreducible`, `modulus_irreducible_rat`, and `quotRing_isDomain`
  prove integral/rational irreducibility and the actual integral quotient's
  domain structure. `Cyclotomic.mul_injective` applies to every nonzero
  generator. `principalMultiplicationEquiv`, `principalMultiplicationBasis`,
  its `_apply` column formula, and `principal_full_rank` give the general
  multiplication basis of `pre.tex`, without a supplied injectivity proof.
- `ModuleSIVP.lean`: `ModuleSIVP.K` is literally
  ℚ[X]/(X^d+1), with its proved field instance. `scalarMap_injective`
  embeds the actual integral quotient via the two coefficient bases.
  `Independent` is field-linear independence of the two embedded vectors;
  `independent_iff` proves equivalence with nonzero integral determinant.
  `normSq`, `vectorNormSq`, and `coefficientVector` use ordinary integral
  coefficients, with `vectorNormSq_eq_coefficients` identifying the 2d norm.
  `completion` is the actual R_d-submodule {(Qa,Ta+gb)} (and hence has an
  underlying additive subgroup). `completionBasis` and
  `completionBasis_columns` prove freeness and identify (Q,T),(0,g).
  `SecondMinimumSq`, `ExactSecondMinimumSq`, and `ModuleSIVPInstance`
  formalize `def:module-sivp`, including explicit basis and nonnegative
  threshold. `exists_minimumDistance` proves attainment by well-ordering
  integral squared norms. `sqNorm_one_monomial` handles both signs, and
  `normSq_one_unit_isometry` proves the integral unit and isometry facts.
  `module_completion` is `lem:module-completion`: given an attained minimum
  δ, the second minimum is exactly Q²+δ, with both attainment and a universal
  bound. `PrincipalCVPInstance.module_exact` connects it to the existing
  coefficient CVP predicate. `completionScale` is exactly
  1+ceilSqrt(max(‖T‖²,‖g‖²)); its strict bounds are proved.
  `x3cToModuleSIVP`, `x3c_module_sivp_yes`, `x3c_module_sivp_no`, and
  `x3c_module_sivp_correct` prove the reduction correctness in
  `thm:module-sivp`, with Γ=Q²+Δ and squared gap Γ versus Γ+4.
- `Fixed.lean`: `Fixed.Triple m` is the powerset-card-3 subtype, and
  `triple_card`/`enumeration` give exactly `m.choose 3` columns.
  `system` is (U_m,1,μ_C). `HasExactCover` uses selected subcollections
  of the input set of triples and counts each element once.
  `cover_iff_feasible` and `hasExactCover_iff_hasSolution` prove the
  combinatorial equivalence, for arbitrary m (not just multiples of 3).
  For m≥3, `baseData`, `generator`, `lattice`, and `basis` depend only on m;
  `data` changes only the mask. `generator_independent` uses the existing
  `generator_mask_independent`; `lattice_independent` identifies the ideals,
  and `basis_columns_independent` identifies all multiplication columns.
  `fixed_family_correct`, `fixed_family_yes`, and `fixed_family_no`
  prove the mathematical content of `thm:fixed-family`, including full rank
  and the Δ_C versus Δ_C+4 gap. Collections here are sets of triples.

## Round 2 changes and size

The canonical `X3CInstance.reductionData`, and hence `x3cToCVP`, now use
`SidonPositions.erdosTuran`. The ambient dimension is O(Mn²), as in the
paper, with the explicit bound above. The formulas for h, t, the parity
penalty, threshold, gap, and the existing correctness theorems are unchanged.
The Sidon formula and prime search are executable; no classical choice is
used to select the prime. The singleton case still has c=0.

The ring connection is now proved, rather than an informal interpretation
of the coefficient model. No ring structure is postulated on the vectors:
the additive bijection and multiplication compatibility connect them directly
to Mathlib's quotient ring. Round 3 adds irreducibility and general full rank;
canonical-embedding norm scaling remains outside this development. The original
compiler-specific full-rank proof via norm estimates is retained as an
independent result. The cyclic lift now also uses the general domain proof.
The cyclic dimension is exactly twice the cyclotomic dimension, and its
squared distances are multiplied by eight.

## Round 3 changes and remaining scope

General full rank, the module-SIVP completion and reduction, and the fixed
masked-family combinatorial reduction are now proved. All new files are
imported by `Hardness.lean`. The dimension-one case is included in the
power-of-two and module theorems. Cyclic full rank needs no supplied
injectivity proof for nonzero power-of-two generators.

The module development works in the genuine integral quotient pairs,
embedded injectively in the genuine rational quotient field pairs. Thus its
independence predicate is over K_d, rather than over ℤ or ℚ. Norms refer to
the coefficient embedding throughout. Exact minima assert both attainment
and the lower bound for every independent pair.

Remaining scope: canonical-embedding norm scaling; rational targets or
arbitrary rational module bases beyond these integral completion instances;
complexity classes, NP membership, machine runtime, CVPP, P/poly and
Karp–Lipton. The fixed family uses sets of triples; no separate indexed
repeated-triple-to-set conversion is formalized. There are no remaining
`sorry` placeholders.

`W_le`, `weight_le`, `cross_card`, and `t_norm` give additional size and norm
calculations. No complexity class, NP-completeness, or machine runtime
assertion is encoded as a theorem; “polynomial” here refers to the explicit
mathematical dimension bounds.
