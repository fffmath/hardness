## Round 1

The model is `Cyclotomic R q`, the quotient of `AddMonoidAlgebra R (ZMod q)`
by the principal ideal of the all-ones element. `IntegerRing q` and
`RationalRing q` specialize it to ℤ and ℚ. The latter permits rational
coefficient denominators; a field instance and the bridge to complex embeddings
remain separate obligations. The construction is defined for any nonzero q;
applications impose primality explicitly.

Paper labels and the current API:

- `lem:cyclotomic-form`: `canonicalForm`, `coeffNormSq_eq`,
  `two_mul_coeffNormSq`, `coeffNormSq_pairs`.
- Coefficient equality/kernel: `mem_cyclotomicIdeal_iff`, `classOf_eq_iff`.
  `convolution` is the cyclic convolution formula. No ideal/ring laws are assumed.
- Constant invariance: `canonicalForm_add_constant_left`,
  `canonicalForm_add_constant_right`, `coeffNormSq_add_constant`,
  `coeffNormSq_of_classOf_eq`, `normSq_classOf`.
- Positivity: `coeffNormSq_nonneg`, `coeffNormSq_eq_zero_iff`,
  `normSq_nonneg`, `normSq_eq_zero_iff` (ℤ and ℚ).
- Conjugation/isometries: `conjugateCoeff`, `conjugate_classOf`,
  `conjugate_conjugate`, `normSq_conjugate`, `normSq_monomial_mul`,
  `normSq_neg_monomial_mul`. `pairNormSq` is the product norm;
  `ramifiedIdeal k` is the actual principal ideal generated by `(1-ζ)^k`.

- `lem:sparse-tools` (i): `sparse_representative` proves the stronger unconditional
  bound `q * supportCard x ≤ 2 * normSq v`. Average the q rows of the integer
  matrix `(z_a-z_b)^2`, choose a row no greater than the average, and subtract
  its indexed coefficient. `sparse_tools_i` gives the paper's rational bound.
  `supportCard_le_sqNorm` reuses `IdealCVP.sqNorm`.
- `lem:second-shell`: `second_shell`; `IsSignedMonomial` means a class equal to
  `±monomial j`, and `normSq_signedMonomial` gives norm q-1. Here this predicate
  names the paper's explicit roots; a classification of all finite-order
  elements of the abstract unit group is not asserted.
- `lem:gauss-sum`: `conjugate_theta`, `theta_mul_conjugate`, `theta_sq`.
  `theta` is the class of Mathlib's integer-valued `quadraticChar`.
  The coefficient correlation is obtained from `jacobiSum_nontrivial_inv`;
  no field/domain instance for the quotient is assumed in this argument.
- Decision problem: `SVPInput`, `encSVP`, `SVPInput.WellFormed`, `SVPYes`,
  `ModuleSVPLang`, `ModuleSVPBits`, `encSVP_mem_iff`. The data shape and encoding
  are reused from `IdealCVP.Complexity.GeneralModule`; all lengths are q-1.
  The two listed generators are columns, so the determinant is
  m₁₁*m₂₂-m₁₂*m₂₁. NP membership is not claimed in this round.

Named hypothesis: `BennettPeikertMinimum : Prop`, exactly the squared Euclidean
minimum bound for every prime q and 1 ≤ k with 2k ≤ q. It is used only as the
explicit parameter `hBP` of `liftedRS_norm_lower`. No global proof or instance
is postulated. Proof attempt: a vector with squared norm < 2k has ℓ₁ norm < 2k;
its zeroth syndrome and 2k ≤ q force its integer coefficient sum to be zero.
The positive and negative exponent multisets then have the same size < k.
Newton identities would equate their elementary symmetric polynomials, hence
root multisets, contradicting nonzero coefficients. Completing this route
requires a characteristic-q Newton-identity/multiset argument with repeated
exponents and factorial denominators below q. This has not been formalized;
the permitted named hypothesis records precisely that remaining input.

a base representative for every syndrome. The complex canonical-embedding
bridge is also pending, as the plan assigns it to a separate file.
